2.2 Weather data
     The weather data are hourly irradiation and
temperature from the CWA Tianzhong station (467270)
[15], 21.4 km from the site, and hourly ERA5 data
obtained through PVGIS [16], [17]. The hour label refers
to the end of the hour (1 MJ/m² per hour equals 277.8
W/m²), and one missing hour was interpolated. The annual
irradiation is 1626 kWh/m². No pyranometer is installed on
the roof, so the irradiance on the modules is not measured.
